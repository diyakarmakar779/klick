hey-os privacy

Last updated 13 October 2026. hey-os is a prototype built for the AI Builder Cup 2026 by Diya and Sivasurya. It helps you meet the right people at events you're already going to, and remember and follow up with them afterwards.

What we read, and only when you ask

  • Google sign-in: your name, email and profile photo, so we know who you are.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): your own events and your answer to each (organiser, going, maybe). We use the access once to import them and do not keep it. We never read other guests' details.
  • Luma: the events you host or attend and your status for each. We use the access once and do not keep it. We never read guest lists, and we never create, change or send anything on Luma.
  • LinkedIn (optional): your name and photo when you sign in with LinkedIn, so you can confirm "Is this you?". Nothing is saved unless you confirm.
  • What you type or record: your profile, what you're looking for at an event, notes, photos and voice notes about people you met.

Who sees what

  • You are hidden from other members unless you switch on "show me to others" for an event. Only other members who also switched it on and are confirmed for the same event can be suggested to you, and you to them.
  • Your contact details are private. They are shared only after both people accept a connection, and only the details you pick.
  • Your notes and memories are private to you. They are shared only if you choose to share one with that person.
  • There is no attendee list. Event pages show counts, and only your own connections by name.

How we use AI

We use Google's Gemini (on Google Cloud) to suggest who to meet and why, to summarise your notes into a memory, and to draft follow-up messages. You review every draft and send it yourself; hey-os never sends messages for you. Your data is not used to train models.

Where it's stored and how to delete it

  • Data is stored on Google Cloud (Firestore and Cloud Storage, Mumbai region) and is only reachable through our server.
  • Disconnecting Calendar or Luma deletes what we imported from it. Deleting a memory deletes its photo or voice note.
  • To delete your account and all your data, email diya@execora.in.

Google user data

hey-os's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
