# EventCard

An event from a connected source, with participation, discoverability and prep progress.

- The date tab is the one brand fill on the card. Participation uses the brand badge; the source is a provenance pill.
- **Participation wording** follows the checklist exactly: Hosting, Invited, Planning to attend, Attended (only when verified), or nothing when unknown.
- The discoverability switch is per event and reversible; its hint says until when.
- Consumer provides: event, source, participation state, discoverability, prep steps done and total.
